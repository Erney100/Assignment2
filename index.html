Spartan Snack Guide 🍓

Cute Campus Cravings at UNCG

Welcome!

This blog highlights some of the best food spots on campus for students who are always hungry between classes. Whether you want something sweet, savory, or caffeinated, there is always a place nearby. These restaurants are perfect for study breaks, friend hangouts, or quick meals. Everything listed here is within walking distance and student-friendly!

Chick-fil-A 🍗

Fast, warm comfort food that never disappoints. Perfect for quick lunches.

Chicken Nuggets

Crispy and golden bite-sized goodness.

Waffle Fries

Salty, crunchy, and iconic.

Frosted Lemonade

Sweet, cold, and super refreshing.

Panda Express 🥡

Big plates, bold flavors, and filling meals for busy days.

Orange Chicken

Sweet, tangy, and crispy favorite.

Chow Mein

Classic noodles with veggies.

Honey Walnut Shrimp

Creamy and slightly sweet.

Starbucks ☕

The ultimate study spot for drinks, snacks, and cozy vibes.

Pink Drink

Fruity, light, and aesthetic.

Caramel Frappuccino

Sweet blended coffee treat.

Bacon Gouda Sandwich

Warm and filling breakfast choice.

Student Tips ✨

Most of these places accept flex, which makes late-night cravings easier. Starbucks is perfect during finals week, and Panda Express is great when you’re super hungry after class.
